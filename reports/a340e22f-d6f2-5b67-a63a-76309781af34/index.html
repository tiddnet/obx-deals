Takeaway

What this does and doesn't tell you

OSH10 isn't underperforming on the metrics we can see — it out-books its Village Realty neighbor and carries the best review record on the block. If the real goal is picking a PM for 2027, though, the comparison is bigger than Village vs. Resort — eight PMCs have real Corolla inventory, and they split into rough groups:

  • Pace Resort Realty, Beach Realty NC, and Brindley Beach show the strongest early 2027 booking pace among PMCs whose detection we trust. Village Realty's own pace can't be fairly judged yet — its Altcha gate is suppressing our ability to detect its real bookings, not necessarily suppressing the bookings themselves.
  • Rate Resort Realty shows the lowest $/bedroom of the eight (n=152) — but mid-session we found Resort Realty's stored rate is missing a real guest fee entirely (section H), so this number is unreliable until that's fixed. Don't read anything into Resort Realty's rate ranking yet.
  • Transparency Village Realty and Outer Banks Blue are the only two of five live-checked PMCs that show a guest nothing but rent and tax. Resort Realty's real guest-fee structure turned out to be confirmable after all (section H) — and it's substantial, $752–$946/week in the properties checked. Worth asking Village Realty whether they'd consider itemizing more clearly, and asking Resort Realty directly what that fee actually covers.
  • Reputation Village Realty sits a tier above Resort Realty in the NotebookLM synthesis (section E) — "Strong" vs. "Polarizing." Once house-condition complaints are set aside as not PM-attributable, though, neither PMC shows a clear service/communication edge over the other in the sourced reviews.
  • Search Village Realty's own page wins the #1 Google spot only 37% of the time for its properties, worst of the block and about half Resort Realty's rate — though neither PMC performs well on OBX-wide generic search terms, so this gap would persist across most plausible switches, not just a Resort Realty move.

Pace leans toward considering a change (once Village Realty's own detection gap is accounted for); rate is currently unreliable pending the fee-storage fix; reputation and search don't clearly favor a Resort Realty move specifically. This isn't a contradiction to resolve in advance — it's the actual shortlist of questions worth putting to Village Realty directly (why the search gap, why the above-market pricing on the weeks it does quote) before assuming any particular PMC on this list is the better bet for 2027.

Before switching anything: pull OSH10's actual 2025/2026 owner statement (gross booked, not list price) and compare it directly against what any prospective PM will quote as a projection — projections run optimistic by nature.
