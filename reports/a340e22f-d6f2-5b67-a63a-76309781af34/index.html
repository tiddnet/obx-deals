PM-level customer reviews

Not this project's own data — a NotebookLM synthesis built from BBB, Trustpilot, Outer Banks Coastal Life, Reddit (r/obx), and Facebook OBX travel/owner groups. Not independently checked by us against those sources; treat it as a well-organized starting point for your own reading, not a verified rating. NotebookLM placed more PMCs than shown here — trimmed to the ones that actually operate in Corolla (per the table at the top of this report), not narrowed to just Village/Resort.

Trimmed to what a PM actually controls: response speed and communication. Physical-house complaints (AC failures, appliance issues, general wear) happen to every PM's inventory regardless of management quality — an AC unit fails on its own schedule, not the PM's — so those are left out here even where the source cited them. Cleaning is left out too: a few bad cleaning reviews turn up for every PM in a large enough sample, and a guest raving about how clean a place was is rare enough that its absence isn't a signal either way. What's kept: response time, communication quality, and how a PM handles a problem once one exists (refund handling, dispute resolution, policy flexibility) — since those are genuinely under the PM's control. Some tier labels below were originally driven partly by the now-removed house-condition detail; read the tier as NotebookLM's overall call, not fully reconstructable from what remains in this row alone.

Takeaway

What this does and doesn't tell you

OSH10 isn't underperforming on the metrics we can see — it out-books its Village Realty neighbor and carries the best review record on the block. If the real goal is picking a PM for 2027, though, the comparison is bigger than Village vs. Resort — eight PMCs have real Corolla inventory, and they split into rough groups:

  • Pace Resort Realty, Beach Realty NC, and Brindley Beach show the strongest early 2027 booking pace among PMCs whose detection we trust. Village Realty's own pace can't be fairly judged yet — its Altcha gate is suppressing our ability to detect its real bookings, not necessarily suppressing the bookings themselves.
  • Rate Resort Realty is excluded from the $/bedroom ranking — its Jun–Aug 2026 data predates a real fee-storage bug we found and fixed this session (section H). The other seven PMCs' numbers aren't affected.
  • Transparency Village Realty and Outer Banks Blue are the only two of five live-checked PMCs that show a guest nothing but rent and tax. Resort Realty's real guest-fee structure turned out to be confirmable after all (section H) — and it's substantial, $752–$946/week in the properties checked. Worth asking Village Realty whether they'd consider itemizing more clearly, and asking Resort Realty directly what that fee actually covers.
  • Reputation Village Realty sits a tier above Resort Realty in the NotebookLM synthesis (section E) — "Strong" vs. "Polarizing." Once house-condition complaints are set aside as not PM-attributable, though, neither PMC shows a clear service/communication edge over the other in the sourced reviews.
  • Search Village Realty's own page wins the #1 Google spot only 37% of the time for its properties, worst of the block and about half Resort Realty's rate — though neither PMC performs well on OBX-wide generic search terms, so this gap would persist across most plausible switches, not just a Resort Realty move.

Pace leans toward considering a change (once Village Realty's own detection gap is accounted for); Resort Realty's rate can't be judged from this data at all; reputation and search don't clearly favor a Resort Realty move specifically. This isn't a contradiction to resolve in advance — it's the actual shortlist of questions worth putting to Village Realty directly (why the search gap, why the above-market pricing on the weeks it does quote) before assuming any particular PMC on this list is the better bet for 2027.

Before switching anything: pull OSH10's actual 2025/2026 owner statement (gross booked, not list price) and compare it directly against what any prospective PM will quote as a projection — projections run optimistic by nature. And weigh reputation and guest-fee transparency alongside the pace/rate numbers, not after them.
